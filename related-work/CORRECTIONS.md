## Substantive clarifications beyond citation formatting

- The sparse start is not new. Its identity with the 2024 Algorithm 2 at chosen bounds is confirmed. The earlier thesis cited there remains bibliographically unverified; it is not silently added to the bibliography.
- SSOM is balanced in expectation, not exactly size preserving. The original 2004 formula was checked visually because text extraction obscured its multiplication sign.
- For the stated AUF, a missing exact cardinality is not necessarily zero: smaller subsets remain feasible. Zero occurs only when the set under the size cap is empty, under P2's declared convention.
- The AUF name and at-most-k formula already occur in FASTENER (2020), Section 5.1. Its printed formula does not establish how empty feasible sets were handled in code.
- Dominance set coverage in Li2025/AsilianBidgoli2020 is not feature-count-axis coverage.
- Xu2025AIMEA is open access under **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**, outside the user's permitted PDF licences. The arXiv v2 of Miyandoab2024 uses the **arXiv perpetual non-exclusive licence**, also outside that list. Neither PDF is in the ZIP.
- Printed issue years are used consistently: SparseEA 2020 (online 2019), MOEA/PSL 2021 (online 2020), PM-MOEA 2022, Improved SparseEA 2023 (online 2021), Enhanced SparseEA 2024 (online 2023), PPI/GPR 2025 (online 2024), and the comparative Miyandoab paper 2026 despite its 2025 DOI string.
