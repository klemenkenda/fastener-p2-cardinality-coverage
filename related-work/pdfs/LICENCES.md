## Why other PDFs are absent

Publicly readable does not mean redistributable under this request. Miyandoab2024 arXiv v2 is non-exclusive arXiv licence; Xu2025AIMEA is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Author manuscripts for Deniz, Li and Oh were used for research but an allowed licence was not established for those specific files. The Xue2016 author manuscript was not bundled merely because Crossref lists a CC BY licence for a version of record: the inspected manuscript's version-specific permission was not established. Other inaccessible or undownloaded papers are supplied as links only. AsilianBidgoli2020's repository XML confirms CC BY 4.0, but the publisher PDF download was blocked; no substitute PDF was manufactured. This is a permitted-PDF selection, not a claim that all other papers are closed access.
