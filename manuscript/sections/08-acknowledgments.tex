We acknowledge the Slovenian National Supercomputing Network (SLING) for access to computing infrastructure. We thank the HPC RIVR consortium and the EuroHPC Joint Undertaking for supporting this research through the provision of Vega computing resources.

We used generative artificial intelligence tools to assist with programming, tracking experiments and hypotheses, evaluations, and preparing draft reports. These tools also assisted with updating the review of related work and organizing the Related Work section. They helped compile information from our experiment records for the paper, including summaries of which methods and datasets were used and descriptions of those methods and datasets, and assisted with producing visualizations in a consistent style. We also used these tools to assist with internal review of the manuscript.
